# Slonix Solutions

**Building skills for the real world.**

Slonix Solutions is an education and training company based in Bengaluru, India, focused on practical learning in technology, engineering, and design. Its learning approach connects technical knowledge with hands-on practice, helping learners develop skills for their career goals.

## About Slonix

Slonix's mission is to make high-quality learning more accessible through technology. With an emphasis on applying concepts, exploring professional tools, and developing project-based skills, Slonix encourages learners to turn curiosity into practical capability.

## Programs

The website presents learning opportunities across four areas:

- **AI & Data:** Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science.
- **Software Development:** Full Stack and MERN Stack Development.
- **Engineering & BIM:** AutoCAD and Revit Architecture.
- **Design & Business:** Digital Marketing and related creative and business skills.

## Project-Based Learning

The website features sample project briefs that illustrate how learning can be applied:

- **Analytics Dashboard:** Explore a dataset and communicate findings through visual insights.
- **Architectural Study:** Develop a studio model and present design choices through organized drawings.
- **Responsive Web Application:** Build a learning planner with accessible navigation and interactive features.

These are illustrative learning examples, rather than a portfolio of completed Slonix or student projects.

## Website Languages

- **JavaScript (JSX):** Interactive features and user interface components.
- **HTML:** Page structure and content.
- **CSS:** Visual design, responsive layouts, and animations.
